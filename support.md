# Support

**Effective date:** 2026-05-13
**App:** CarContractScan
**Operator:** Fractalthink

CarContractScan is published by Fractalthink. We respond to every support email we receive. The fastest way to reach us is the email address below.

## Email

`support@fractalthink.com`

Expected response window: within two business days (Pacific Time, Monday through Friday).

## What to include

Two pieces of information help us respond quickly:

- **Your Support ID.** Open the app, tap the gear icon, then tap **Support ID** under "Support". The ID is copied to your clipboard. This lets us find the right purchase and scan history without you having to share an account — CarContractScan does not have accounts.
- **The app version.** Visible at the bottom of the Settings screen, for example "App version 1.0.0 (build 42)".

A brief description of what happened, with the time of the scan if you remember it, helps us correlate to our server logs.

## Refunds

Scan credits are purchased through the App Store or Google Play. Refunds are processed by Apple or Google directly, not by Fractalthink.

- **iOS:** Apple processes refunds at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **Android:** Google processes refunds via the Play Store app under Account → Purchase history.

If a store denies a refund you believe should be granted, email `support@fractalthink.com` with your Support ID and a screenshot of the denial. We can sometimes issue replacement credits directly.

## Account deletion

To delete your account and all associated scan history, open the app and go to **Settings → Delete account**. The deletion is immediate and cannot be reversed. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for what gets removed.
